Comandos que transformam uma foto

Uma foto só, vários resultados. Comandos de imagem pro ChatGPT e prompts de vídeo pro Magnific, prontos pra copiar.

como usar

Abre o ChatGPT, anexa a foto e digita só o comando. Toca no comando pra copiar.

Se o resultado não vier do jeito que você imaginou, escreve junto o que você quer, numa frase. Ex.: /miniature, mantém meu rosto igual.

Prompts de vídeo no Magnific

No Magnific, anexa a imagem, cola o prompt e gera. Pode deixar em inglês, funciona melhor assim.

adesivo
A hand pinches the top of the printed portrait photo held in hands and peels it downward off the frame, revealing the city street scene underneath, realistic paper peel with slight bend, static camera, same lighting
volta 360°
Smooth 360 degree orbit shot around the person, starting from the front, the camera circles all the way around at eye level and returns to the front, the person stays still and natural, consistent face and outfit, realistic
tempo congelado
The woman walks naturally along the sidewalk holding her latte. Suddenly she trips on the pavement and stumbles forward, and the coffee flies out of the cup. The moment of the splash turns into extreme slow motion, almost frozen: the coffee splash and droplets hang suspended in the air while the camera keeps moving slowly toward her side. Realistic, cinematic, normal lens, same woman and same street.
derretendo
Static locked camera, no zoom, no pan. The thick melted cheese keeps flowing and dripping continuously down from the burger at the top of the frame, running down over the black Instagram UI bar with the icons and caption, pooling and splattering into the cartoon character's open mouth at the bottom. Realistic viscous cheese physics, slow continuous drip, everything else in the frame stays completely still like two stacked photos in a feed, only the cheese moves.

Dica: nos prompts de vídeo, troca "latte", "burger" ou "woman" pelo que aparece na sua foto.
